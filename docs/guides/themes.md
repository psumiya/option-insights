# Themes and layout

## Light and dark

The app follows your system's light or dark setting until you choose one with the sun/moon button in the header. Your choice is remembered in this browser.

![The report in dark mode]({{ '/assets/images/screenshots/theme-dark.png' | relative_url }})

## Phones and narrow screens

On a narrow screen the chapter rail becomes a row of chips along the top of the report, and charts resize to the width available. See the [Report]({{ '/views/report/' | relative_url }}#navigating-the-report) page for a phone screenshot.
